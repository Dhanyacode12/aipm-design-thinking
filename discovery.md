## 4. Persona, Journey Map, POV, HMW

### Persona (working model, not a biography)

**Group:** mid-bootcamp Data/AI PM students applying for a first job. *(scope given by the AI Project Manager)*

- **Goal:** get a first job. *(scope)*
- **Behaviours:** search jobs on a platform (E1); decide by ease of applying and CV fit (E4, E5); tailor the CV with AI tools such as Enhancv, Overleaf and Claude (E6, E9, E11); try to connect with people related to the job (E8). *(Observed)*
- **Constraints:** must meet advanced requirements; German speaking may be a barrier (E10). *(Observed)*
- **Pain points:** misleading level labels (E3); duplicates (E2); repeated ATS responses despite effort (E12). *(Observed, each single-source)*
- **Feelings:** relieved, hopeful, stressed (E7); frustrated (E12). *(Observed)*
- **Open questions:** how do they choose which jobs deserve tailoring? How many applications do they send? Do other students feel the same? What do they do after an ATS rejection? What do they think while reading requirements?

### Journey map (long-shot, 5 phases)

Actor: mid-bootcamp student. Scenario: applying for a first job. Goal: get responses to applications.

| Phase | Action | Thought | Emotion | Evidence / assumption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Find jobs | Search by job titles on a job platform | Unknown | Unknown | E1 |
| 2. Judge the ad | Notice duplicates and level mismatch; check requirements and language needs | Unknown | Unknown | E2, E3, E10 |
| 3. Decide to apply | Apply directly if easy; save for later if it needs more | Unknown | Unknown | E4, E5 |
| 4. Tailor CV | Use AI tools and CV editors to match keywords; connect with people | Unknown | Stress (assumption, linked to E7) | E6, E8, E9, E11 |
| 5. Wait for response | Receive ATS responses | Unknown | Frustration | E12 |

- **Pain point:** after careful tailoring, ATS responses keep coming back negative (E12). Single source.
- **Opportunity:** help students judge whether a job is worth the tailoring effort before they invest it (inferred from E3, E4, E12). This is an opportunity to explore, not a feature.
- **Gaps:** the "Thought" column is Unknown in every phase because the interview notes recorded actions and outcomes, not thoughts. Emotion is only evidenced for the whole process (E7) and for phase 5 (E12).

### Problem statement (draft)

Mid-bootcamp Data/AI PM students applying for a first job put effort into tailoring their CV for jobs without knowing whether the effort will lead to a response, while job ads can be misleading about the level required. *(Inferred from I1, I2, I4)*

### POV (HYPOTHESIS, not a finding)

Evidence-auditor warning (accepted): the premise that students are uncertain whether tailoring pays off has no direct evidence. The evidence supports only: two students, one frustrated by repeated ATS responses (E12, lower confidence), one noting misleading ad levels (E3). The prototype is meant to test this hypothesis.

Mid-bootcamp Data/AI PM students need a way to judge whether a job is worth their application effort, because job ads can be misleading about seniority (E3) and careful tailoring can still end in repeated ATS rejection (E12). *(Inferred; rests on single-source evidence)*

### HMW (HYPOTHESIS, not a finding)

How might we help mid-bootcamp students decide which jobs are worth tailoring a CV for, when job ads can be misleading and the outcome is uncertain?
